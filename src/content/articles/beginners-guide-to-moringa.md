## Get to know the plant

Moringa is the common name used for Moringa oleifera in this guide. You may encounter leaf foods, dried leaf powders, infusions and concentrated supplements. These are different preparations: a claim about one should not automatically be applied to another.

## Start with the label

Before choosing a product, check the plant part, the ingredient list and the supplier’s preparation instructions. Ask how the product is sourced and stored. A familiar plant name does not tell you everything about what is in the package.

## Traditional use

Plant traditions can offer valuable cultural context. They do not, by themselves, establish effectiveness for a medical condition. We will add more specific historical accounts only after checking sources and context.

## Nutrition and evidence

This guide does not assign nutrient quantities or health outcomes to a cup of moringa tea. Product preparation and quantities matter. Research summaries and nutrition figures are reserved for a future, sourced editorial review.

## Questions worth asking

- Which plant part is used?
- Is this a food product or a concentrated supplement?
- What preparation and storage instructions apply?
- Are there suitability warnings or other ingredients?

## Safety first

Talk with a qualified healthcare professional if you take medication or have questions about suitability. Do not change prescribed treatment based on a plant guide.
