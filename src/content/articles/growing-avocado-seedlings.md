## Choose for your location

Before selecting an avocado seedling, describe your site, available space, soil and water supply to a nursery or local extension adviser. Ask for advice that applies to your location rather than a universal growing schedule.

## Ask about the plant

Confirm cultivar, propagation method and whether the plant is grafted. Ask about source, health and any rootstock information. These details affect planning and expectations.

## Plan the mature tree

An attractive young tree still needs a suitable long-term growing space. Seek cultivar-specific advice on spacing, pollination, drainage and care before planting.

## Do not buy a yield promise

Time to fruiting and crop performance vary. Ask what assumptions sit behind any estimate, and avoid treating it as a guarantee.

## Keep local guidance close

Record the supplier’s instructions and consult a qualified agricultural adviser about problems. Precise agronomic figures will be added to this guide only after locally applicable sources are reviewed.
