## Begin with a clearly labelled product

Choose a product intended for food use, with an identifiable ingredient list and preparation instructions. Do not brew a plant from a photograph or common name alone.

## Follow the preparation instructions

Use the quantity, water temperature and steeping time on the confirmed label. Different plants and preparations are not interchangeable, so this guide does not prescribe one universal recipe or medicinal dose.

## Make the ritual your own

Set aside a clean cup and a few quiet minutes. Notice the aroma and flavour. If you add another ingredient, consider its suitability and any allergies too.

## Store with care

Keep the original packaging or a clear label, and follow storage and expiry instructions. Ask the supplier if those details are missing.

## Keep wellness claims in perspective

A pleasant tea ritual is not a treatment plan. If you are using herbs for a health concern, discuss that concern with a qualified healthcare professional.
