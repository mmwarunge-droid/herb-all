## One plant, different preparations

Ginger is associated here with Zingiber officinale. You might encounter fresh rhizome in cooking, dried ingredients in a tea, or concentrated extracts. These products should not be treated as equivalent.

## Look beyond the product name

Check the plant part, full ingredient list and instructions. A blend may contain more than ginger, and a supplement may have a different concentration from a culinary preparation.

## Traditional use and research

Traditional practices and clinical studies answer different questions. A reliable research summary needs to explain what was studied and how it compares with the actual product. We are not making a treatment claim in this introductory guide.

## Decide with care

Ask a healthcare professional about suitability when medicines, pregnancy, breastfeeding, children or ongoing health conditions are involved. Follow verified product instructions rather than an unsourced dosage online.
